ABOUT US

Money knowledge, made clearer.

Fynorix is a finance and investing education website intended to help readers understand markets, personal finance, investing concepts and practical planning tools.

Our goal is to publish clear explainers, thoughtful context and useful calculators for an international, English-speaking audience. We aim to help readers ask better questions and make more informed decisions.

Publisher

Fynorix is published by Subhash Dissanayake. For editorial questions, corrections or general enquiries, contact Fynorixfinance@gmail.com.

How we work

  • We aim to use understandable language and distinguish educational examples from real market data.
  • We expect contributors to use original writing and check important factual claims against reliable sources.
  • We explain that investments can lose value and do not promise financial outcomes.
  • We identify advertising and commercial relationships when applicable.

What Fynorix is not

Fynorix is not a bank, broker, investment adviser or tax/legal professional. The website does not access brokerage accounts or provide personalized investment recommendations.
