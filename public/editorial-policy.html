EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

A practical starting point for the site's real editorial process.

Owner action required: Keep only standards the site can genuinely follow. Add a named editor or contact route before launch.

Originality

Articles should be written for readers rather than search engines alone. We aim to explain what a reader can learn, how the information applies, and what its limits are. Copied, lightly rewritten or bulk-generated low-value content should not be published.

Sources and verification

Writers should prefer primary sources for market rules, official statistics, regulatory notices and product disclosures. Check dates, jurisdiction, units and relevant limitations. Link to sources when practical and identify assumptions in examples.

Risk and balance

Finance content should not promise returns, portray speculative assets as risk-free or pressure readers into a transaction. Explain relevant risks and distinguish facts from opinion. Articles are educational, not personal recommendations.

Images and AI assistance

Use images the site owns, has permission to use, or has generated under terms permitting the intended use. Avoid misleading image captions. Review AI-assisted text for accuracy, originality, tone and missing context before publishing.

Corrections and updates

Correct material errors promptly and update time-sensitive stories when facts materially change. Add a visible correction note for significant changes. Use dates carefully and do not present a sample price as a live quote.

Advertising and conflicts

Clearly identify sponsored content, affiliate links and other material commercial relationships. Advertising must not determine an article's conclusions. Ads should not be styled to trick readers into clicking or placed within controls.
